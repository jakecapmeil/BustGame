    How to play

    The goal

    Wipe every other colour off the board. Last colour standing wins.

    Opening

    Everyone picks one tile on the empty board. It busts immediately, throwing a ball to each of its four neighbours — so you start with up to four tiles.

    Openings claim a 3×3 zone and no two zones may overlap, so nobody can crowd anyone out on move one. Shaded tiles are the ones you can't take.

    Your turn

    Tap any tile you own to add a ball. Tiles hold three.

    Busting

    Add a ball to a tile that already holds three and it busts: the tile empties and fires one ball into each of the four neighbouring tiles. Every tile a ball lands on becomes yours.

    If a landing ball pushes a tile past three, that one busts too — chains can run away across the whole board.

    The edge tax

    A bust always fires four balls, even with nowhere to put them. Bust on an edge and one ball falls off the board; bust in a corner and you lose two. Edges are cheap to hold and expensive to attack from.

    Reading a tile

    The dots inside a disc are its balls. A disc with a ring around it is loaded — one more ball and it goes.
