        How to play

        The goal

        Wipe every other colour off the board. Last side standing wins.

        Opening

        Everyone picks one tile on the empty board. It busts immediately, throwing a ball to each of its four neighbours — so you start with up to four tiles.

        Openings claim a 3×3 zone and no two zones may overlap, so nobody can crowd anyone out on move one. Reach for a tile inside someone's zone and it flashes red at you.

        Your turn

        Tap any tile you own to add a ball. Tiles hold three.

        Busting

        Add a ball to a tile that already holds three and it busts: the tile empties and fires one ball into each of the four neighbouring tiles. Every tile a ball lands on becomes yours.

        If a landing ball pushes a tile past three, that one busts too — chains can run away across the whole board.

        The edge tax

        A bust always fires four balls, even with nowhere to put them. Bust on an edge and one ball falls off the board; bust in a corner and you lose two. Walls eat balls the same way. Edges are cheap to hold and expensive to attack from.

        Teams

        In Duos your balls reinforce a partner's tile instead of stealing it, and your side only loses when both of you are wiped out.

        Reading a tile

        The dots inside a disc are its balls. A disc with a ring around it is loaded — one more ball and it goes.
